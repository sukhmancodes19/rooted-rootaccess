Coarse Single-Station Averaging

  • One city-wide number: Tells millions of citizens their air is the same, whether they are beside an open biomass fire or in a green residential park.

By separating the map into two layers—a muted base layer without labels underneath the H3 hexagons, and a dedicated labels reference layer rendered in a custom pane at z-index 650 above the hexagons—street and locality names remain crisp and legible with zero watermarks while allowing hexagon hover tooltips to function seamlessly.
By fusing ground-truth observations from official CPCB monitoring stations with high-resolution Open-Meteo atmospheric features (boundary layer height, humidity, wind vectors, and surface pressure), the trained LightGBM model learns physical transport relationships to infer PM2.5 levels across 0.7 km² neighborhood blocks.
Pehredar includes a dual-tier conversational pipeline: Gemini provides high-precision health triage and falls back to ultra-low latency Groq inference during network congestion. If connectivity drops entirely, the built-in offline demo mode serves pre-computed spatial arrays without interruption.
